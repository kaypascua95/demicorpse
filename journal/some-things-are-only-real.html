Journal No. 001 · Massachusetts

Some things are only real after you write them down.

On keeping evidence of a life while it is still happening.

I keep thinking about how much of a life disappears without ceremony.

Not the big things. We are good at keeping those. Birthdays get photographs. Trips get albums. Milestones become dates we can point to.

It is everything between them that vanishes.

The light in a room at six in the evening. A sentence somebody said that stayed with me for reasons I couldn't explain. The game I couldn't stop playing. A screenshot. A half-finished thought. The version of myself who existed for three months and then quietly became somebody else.

I don't want an archive of accomplishments. I want an archive of being here.

So this is DemiCorpse now. Not a portfolio pretending my life has a clean narrative. Not quite a blog either. A place to leave pieces behind while they're still warm.

Somewhere between alive and archived.
